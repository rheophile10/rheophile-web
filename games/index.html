rheopanzer bring your own assets

Panzer General 2 rebuilt as data: the whole game state is a fold over its moves, so turns sync peer to peer over WebRTC. Combat forecasts that show their arithmetic, leaders, overstrength, and a soundtrack of deeply inappropriate clown music. A demo scenario plays straight away; for the PG2 scenarios and campaigns, drop your own asset bundle on the page.
